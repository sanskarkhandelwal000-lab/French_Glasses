## Marketplace photography guidance

Amazon's public photography guide recommends white backgrounds, accurate colours, clear focus, multiple angles and a product occupying most of the frame. Its image requirements keep the main product image free of added text, graphics, logos or misleading props. Place feature graphics and lifestyle shots in secondary gallery slots, subject to category rules. Amazon main images are expected to represent the actual product photographically; these generated reconstructions should not be represented as marketplace-approved.

Sources: https://sell.amazon.com/blog/product-photos and https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1881?locale=en_us .
