# Image edit prompt set

Mode: built-in `image_gen`, using each original product photograph as the edit target/reference. All assets are square, photorealistic, free of captions and logos, with premium optical-product lighting.

## Master directions

- **Studio front:** preserve the product's visible silhouette, finish, lens tone, bridge, and visible modules; remove hands, packaging, counters, text and reflections; present a straight-on, fully visible product on a warm off-white seamless with diffused light, gentle contact shadow, and even margins.
- **Studio angle/detail:** retain the same product identity and colors while showing a restrained three-quarter angle for the round frames or a close front detail for the shield and clear-lens frames; emphasize visible construction only, with no invented controls, ports, or claims.
- **Lifestyle:** show an adult model wearing that exact frame at believable scale in a natural setting, with the glasses prominent, premium editorial light and realistic skin; no tech overlays or unsupported feature demonstrations.

## Product-specific anchors

- **Glossy round black:** glossy sculpted black frame; softly angular round dark lenses; circular modules on both outer front corners. Café/street lifestyle.
- **Square black clear:** satin black broad rectangular frame; genuinely clear lenses; round corner details, one with a warm metal ring. Modern workspace lifestyle.
- **Matte round black:** matte black softly rounded frame; dark lenses; circular outer-corner details. City-street lifestyle.
- **Orange shield:** bright orange-red frame; orange-gold mirror shield; angular nose cutout; single central dark circular module. Post-ride road lifestyle.
- **Black shield:** black wraparound frame; neutral dark smoke shield; central dark circular module. Park-running lifestyle.
- **White/blue shield:** white wraparound frame; blue iridescent shield; blue central brow with one black circular module and white nose piece. Coastal cycling lifestyle.

The orange secondary image was revised from a three-quarter draft to a front detail because the draft did not show its central module. The white/blue front image was revised to remove an extra small mark the generator added beneath the central module.
