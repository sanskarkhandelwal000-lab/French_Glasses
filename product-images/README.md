## Use on the website

Each folder contains exactly three 1254 × 1254 PNGs:

1. `01-studio-front.png`: product category tile, search results, and first product-page image. Keep the whole frame visible; avoid a tight automated crop.
2. `02-studio-angle.png` or `02-studio-detail.png`: second product-page image. Shows depth or a visible hardware/lens detail.
3. `03-lifestyle.png`: third product-page image. Shows the scale and appearance when worn. These are generated model scenes, not documentary photographs.

For a complete product-page gallery, aim for **5–7 images per actual SKU**. Add genuine photos of (4) a full side/temple view, (5) rear/nose-pad and folded view, (6) exact shipped contents, and (7) fit dimensions or a real sample photo/video if recording quality is a selling point. Use the real product for those additional views so hinges, controls, charging interfaces, and accessories are accurate. For color swatches, use the front image of each colorway and link directly to that SKU.

## Product facts to check before launch

The three shield reference sheets say “M95” and depict a central circular module. They also print claims about a camera, Bluetooth calling/music, AI interaction/translation, Wi-Fi transfer, 49 g weight, and a 290 mAh battery. These are **supplier-sheet claims**, not verified specifications for the stock in hand. Confirm the exact model, camera resolution and recording format, battery, connectivity, app support, lens protection, fit dimensions, included accessories, and warranty with a physical sample or manufacturer's documentation before adding badges, copy, or feature graphics. The two round frames and clear-lens rectangular frame likewise need their model identities and functions confirmed.

Avoid using generated reflections or lifestyle scenes as evidence of real optical performance or recording quality. The square studio images are the most useful starting assets for the category list; review product geometry and color against stock samples before publication.
